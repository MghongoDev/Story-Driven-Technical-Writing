# Source Articles

The 13 articles this reference was built from, with one-line descriptions. All
published on DEV Community by David Mwandairo.

## SQL series

1. [DDL and DML: how a database designs a room and remembers who escaped it](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/ddl-and-dml-how-a-database-designs-a-room-and-remembers-who-escaped-it-ogf):
   DDL vs DML taught through a two-person escape room studio, including the
   written-vs-execution order of a SELECT.

2. [SQL Joins Explained: A Beekeeping Co-op in Six Queries](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/sql-joins-explained-a-beekeeping-co-op-in-six-queries-3j1j):
   All six join types, each introduced by what happens to an unmatched row, against a
   co-op dataset with three deliberate cracks.

3. [Subqueries and CTEs: Asking a Question Inside a Question](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/subqueries-and-ctes-asking-a-question-inside-a-question-3lpk):
   Scalar, IN, correlated, and derived-table subqueries, then CTEs, then a recursive
   CTE walking a three-generation mentorship chain.

4. [SQL Functions: The Toolbox Hiding Inside Every SELECT](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/sql-functions-the-toolbox-hiding-inside-every-select-3m5c):
   Scalar, aggregate, window, date, and conditional functions classified by behavior,
   demonstrated on a messy free-text notes column.

5. [Window Functions: Seeing the Group Without Losing the Row](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/window-functions-seeing-the-group-without-losing-the-row-4d3e):
   ROW_NUMBER, RANK, DENSE_RANK, NTILE, LAG/LEAD, and frames, staged on a harvests
   table expanded to give each hive a season.

6. [Handling NULL Values in SQL](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/handling-null-values-in-sql-42fl):
   NULL as "we don't know," taught through a record shop's unlabeled sleeves, with
   COALESCE, IFNULL/ISNULL, and NULLIF each solving a named problem.

## Project articles

7. [The SafariConnect Data Story](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/the-safariconnect-data-story-3dbl):
   290 rows of tangled booking data cleaned in PostgreSQL staging and turned into six
   boardroom answers.

8. [Building the SafariConnect Dashboard](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/building-the-safariconnect-dashboard-4odl):
   The next day: five Power BI pages, the DAX behind each metric, and a public list
   of what the finished dashboard got wrong.

9. [Cleaning and Analyzing Tembo Hotel's Bookings](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/cleaning-and-analyzing-tembo-hotels-bookings-41o5):
   A full staging → audit → fix → CHECK-constrain → analyze pipeline on 286 hotel
   bookings, with a witness-column method for ambiguous dates.

10. [Building the Tembo Hotel Dashboard in Power BI](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/building-the-tembo-hotel-dashboard-in-power-bi-cj6):
    Three dashboard pages graded against three stated tests, including a planted
    mistake the reader is invited to find.

## Python series

11. [Python Basics in the Matatu Context](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/python-basics-in-the-matatu-context-165a):
    Variables, the four core data types, I/O, and syntax, taught by a matatu
    conductor, closing with three runnable scripts.

12. [Python Operators and Conditionals at the Airport Check-in Desk](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/python-operators-and-conditionals-at-the-airport-check-in-desk-1h2p):
    Arithmetic, comparison, and logical operators plus if/elif/else, staged as one
    passenger's bag going over a scale.

13. [Python Loops at the Nairobi Animal Orphanage](https://dev.to/david_mwandairo/python-loops-at-the-nairobi-animal-orphanage-1fkn):
    for, while, break, continue, and enumerate(), animated by a ranger's morning
    checks and an eight-enclosure round that uses all four at once.
